> You can deduct any reasonable current expense incurred to earn business income
> - Gov of Canada

Current Expense
- current: deducted the year you incurred it
- benefit of < 1 year
- eg: maintenance, repair, restore
	- eg: property back to original form
	- repairing computer 
	- phone bill

capital expense
- deducted over the useful life of that purchase over a few years
- benefit of 1+ years
- eg: improve property
- purchase computer
- replace wood stairs with concrete stairs
- new phone

## Deductions

When is an expense reasonable
- software developer: can deduct internet costs
	- not reasonable for construction contractor to do the same

Personal + business expense: eg: phone or car: need to keep track of what portion is used for business (Mile IQ)
- commute doesn't count, treated the same as an employee

Exceptions
- meals and entertainment: still only 50% deductible
- 6 events per year we can deduct 100% (need to invite everyone from the office)

Vehicle related deductions

Home office expenses
- reasonable allocation

Gym and golf memberships: specifically excluded
