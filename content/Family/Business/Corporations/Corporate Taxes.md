## Active income
- Canadian controlled private corporation
- small business deduction
	- 0 -> 500k
	- 9% federally
	- 2% in Alberta
	- [8.3% -> 3.2% in Ontario](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/corporations/provincial-territorial-corporation-tax/ontario-provincial-corporation-tax/ontario-small-business-deduction.html)
		- line 402
- General tax rate
	- a bit higher

## Passive income

- Federal: 38.7% 
- Alberta: 8%
- Ontario: ?%

Investment income
- rental properties
	- taxed at that rate
- capital gains
	- half is taxed at that rate
	- other half: CDA: Capital dividend account
		- tax free
		- notional account that keeps track of dividends that can be paid to shareholders
- dividends
	- 38.3%
	- some of that was already taxed by the corporation that gave that dividend

## Integration
- make things equal for someone with a job and someone with a corporation
- low tax rate for an active corporation
	- they can spend that extra money on passive income
	- they get taxed higher on that passive income
	- "levels out"
