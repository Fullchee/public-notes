## Premium

>[!What is a premium]-
> - recurring (monthly or annual) fee for the insurance

## Deductible

>[!What is a deductible]-
> - amount you need to pay before insurance kicks in
> 	- eg: $250 annual deductible
> - the higher the deductible, the lower the premium

## Coinsurance

aka co-insurance


>[!What is co-insurance?]-
>- percentage you have to pay of the bill after paying your deductible
>- eg: 20%
>	- insurance will pay 80%, you pay 20%

## Copay
>[!What is a copay?]-
>- fixed amount you pay at the time of service


## Example
- Joe gets stitches at the hospital on Jan 1
	- $1000 hospital bill
	- Joe pays $250 deductible since it's a new fiscal year
	- then he pays 20% of the rest (20% of 750 = $150) and insurance covers the rest
	- so he paid $400 and insurance paid $600
- prescription for pain killer
	- copay for $30
- goes to family doctor to remove stitches in 30 days
	- copay for $100
- gets a $2000 MRI
	- already paid deductible
	- he pays 20% ($400) and insurance pays the rest
