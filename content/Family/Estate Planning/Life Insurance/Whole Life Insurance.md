## Why life insurance

- leave money behind for family members
- income replacement insurance

## Whole vs term life insurance

- flat monthly premium for a set term
	- eg: $100/month for term life
	- 10, 20, 30 years
	- monthly premium is much lower than 
- end of the term
	- premiums jump
	- people usually cancel policy at that point

- $500/month for whole life
	- part of it goes to insurance
	- part of it goes to "cash value" investment account
		- 4-6% a year
			- only to the cash value, not the whole premium
		- closer to ~2% after fees and commissions and insurance
		- offset the increasing cost of insurance as you get older
		- so your premiums stay flat

- if you die, they pay you the life insurance and they keep the savings
- if you cash out savings, they cancel the life insurance
