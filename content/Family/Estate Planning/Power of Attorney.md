Before you pass away

If you become incapacitated, someone can act on your behalf

Without a POA, your family may have to go to court to pay your bills (access your bank accounts)


## Types of POA

### Non continuing POA for property
- if you're out of country
- revoked if you're incapacitated

### Continuing POA for property
- active when you're incapacitated
- pay your bills
- managing your investments
- applying for disability
- collect pension
