## Probate
- Probate fee
	- ~1.5% of your total assets
- some things are excluded from probate
	- shares of a private corporation
	- need a second will that's dated at the same time
		- so it doesn't override
- stuff like private corporate shares
- art collections
- The second will's executor has to be a different person?
- How to make sure that the second will doesn't conflict with the first will

Once a will goes through probate, it becomes public

### Naming beneficiaries

- you can name a beneficiary for your financial accounts
	- bypass probate fee: 1.5%
- make sure to name someone and not your estate

#### Which accounts can you name beneficiaries
- RRSP, TFSA
- investment accounts in insurance company
	- is this just whole life insurance?

## Trusts

- corporations or sizeable or real estate portfolios
- useful for estate freeze
	- selling common shares to family member in exchange for preferred shares
	- you retain control of corporation while younger family members buy into the business
- high setup and maintenance cost
