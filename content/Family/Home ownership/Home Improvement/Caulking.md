## Tools & Materials
- knife to strip the old caulk
	- utility knife
	- razor blade
	- putty knife
- grouting sponge (scrub daddy?)
- alcohol 
- painter's tape
- caulk
- caulk gun

### Which caulk to use
- 100% silicon by GE
	- for surfaces you won't paint like bathtubs
	- I ended up getting Mono Silicone Pro (10 years)
		- instead of max (20 years)
		- GE was printable silicon for some reason? 

---
## Remove the existing caulk
1. Block the drain
	1. so that old stuff doesn't fall into the drain
	2. REMOVE EVERYTHING??
2. Strip the old caulk
3. clean up bathtub
4. Painter's tape at 1/4 inch away
5. Fill bathtub with water
	1. so that when the water is drained, the caulk is compressed
6. Cut the caulk tip at 45 degrees
7. Caulk at 45 degrees
8. press with finger
9. Tear off the tape while the caulk is wet
10. let it cure for 24 hours
11. don't get it wet for 30 mins
