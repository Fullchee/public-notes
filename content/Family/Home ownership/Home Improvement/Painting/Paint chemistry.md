## Solvent
- water or oil

- Solvent: evaporates and leaves the paint film behind
	- usually water
- Polymer
	- usually acrylic
		- sometimes has polyurethane dispersions
	- resins that bind the paint to the application surface
	- makes it scrubbable, washable
- pigments (colour)
	- titanium dioxide
