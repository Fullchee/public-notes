> [!What is clutter in motion]-
> Clutter that's tolerable or cozy
> eg: books you're reading on the kitchen counter
> 
> can become clutter in stasis


>[!What is Clutter in stasis]-
>Clutter that's been out of place for so long that you treat it like it's not there
>
>Treated like a permanent fixture
>working around it, shuffling it to the side when it's in the way
