## Outside

- Lube garage door
- basement window wells (fall and spring)
	- buy a cover so you don't have to clean it up every season

- Spring
	- Clean gutters
	- Dryer vent clean
	- AC maintenance
		- kick start fan
		- check for mouse nest
		- (spray with hose) to clean out condenser coil

- Summer
	- Clean and stain deck

- Fall
	- Clean gutters, clear out leaves
	- Check weather stripping (around door and bottom)
	- lawn mower maintenance (after the grass stops growing)
		- change spark plug
		- replace air filter
		- lube
		- ensure everything is tight
		- wire brush: clean out the gunk under 
		- change oil
		- sharpen blade

- Winter
	- prune trees
	- lawn mower: replace air filter, spark plug, change oil, sharpening blade


## Everywhere

- Doors
	- lube hinges and ensure they're tightened (so they stay aligned)
		- or replace with 2.25" screws
- test smoke and CO detectors (monthly)
- test GFCI outlets (monthly)
	- window screens (spring)
- ceiling fans: tighten the fans (so they aren't drooping)

## Basement

- Replace furnace filter (3 months)
- Fall
	- Water heater (annual)
- Ensure main shut-off valve works
	- test water at the lowest level and ensure it stops
- Test sump pump
## Kitchen

- Fridge coils
	- at least once before summer

## Bathroom

- check caulk lines (spring)
- check under bathroom sink/under vanities for leaks (monthly)
- cruft in toilet tank: any acid (barkeeper's friend, citric acid)
- Winter
	- clean exhaust fan
