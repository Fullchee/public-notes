## What title insurance covers

* title issues that prevent you from having clear ownership of property
* previous home buyer's unpaid bills
* encroachment issues
	* structure on your property needs to be removed becase
* [[Title Fraud]]
* Errors in surveys and public records
* other title related issues


Other benefits
- allow deals to close in a quicker time frame

## Title search
- everything that's registered up to that point
- doesn't show anything unregistered
	- if they haven't paid their property taxes or other bills in the last 90 days
