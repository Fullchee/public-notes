## [[Non owner car insurance]]

## Insurance required in Ontario

>[!question]- Liability insurance
>- when you're at fault: pays others' damages
>- mandatory minimum $200k in Ontario
>	- recommended $1-2 million
>	- ideally: maximize your liability coverage?
>	- it should cost like ~$5/month for a ton more coverage

#### Bodily injury liability
- lawsuits from medical expenses, pain & suffering, lost wages
- personal injury max: $250k
- total injuries per accident: $500k

#### Property damage liability
- lawsuits for damaged property
- property max: $100k

### Uninsured automobile coverage
- cover your body and car's injuries & death by uninsured driver or hit & run
- acts like the other driver's liability insurance


###  Direct compensation property damage
- not at fault & person at fault has insurance -> pay for damage of your vehicle from accident
- mandatory in Ontario

## Non mandatory car insurance

### Comprehensive coverage
- applies when there's no other vehicle involved
	- usually required when your vehicle is leased or financed
- understandably not a thing in [[Non owner car insurance]]
- example coverage
	- fire
	- theft
	- falling objects
	- riots
	- storms or floods
	- impact with bird/deer/animal
	- vandalism
- INCREASE YOUR DEDUCTIBLE, especially if you have an emergency fund
	- save a ton of money


### Collision coverage
- you're at fault -> cover damage to your own vehicle
	- required if vehicle is leased or financed


### Loss of use/rental coverage

- rental car costs while your vehicle gets replaced because of a claim


### Waiver of depreciation

- replace your new vehicle in the event of a total loss to the vehicle or theft of the vehicle
- pay the original purchase price to fully replace the vehicle following a loss
- only available right when you buy a new car 
- otherwise, insurance companies will only cover the depreciated value of the car

### Liability damage to non-owned vehicles
- your coverage also works on a rental car
	- only valid in Canada & US

### Accident forgiveness
- protect driving record with insurance company in the event of an at-fault accident
	- I assume just that current company though?
- the insurance company may keep you
	- so you don't have to buy expensive high-risk insurance
