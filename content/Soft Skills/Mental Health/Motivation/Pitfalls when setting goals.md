# Compensatory goal

>[!Example of compensatory goal]-
>1. fail a class
>2. sign up for it again
>3. "I have to get an A"

>[!Why are compensatory goals ineffective?]-
>all or nothing, perfectionistic
>sets us up to quit
>struggling to do 2 things at once
1. make up for lost time
2. victory that's so great that balances failure
3. 2 goals in one
	1. original goal of passing the class
	2. compensating for the past
		1. fix an internal emotion
4. goal is perfectionistic in nature
	1. all or nothing
5. sets us up to quit


# Fantasy based goal

>[!Example of fantasy goal]-
>6 pack by summer
>millionaire by 30


>[!Problem with fantasy goal]-
>it's too high in the sky
>eg: climbing Mt Everest: no visible progress after 4 hours of climbing
>"getting in shape"
